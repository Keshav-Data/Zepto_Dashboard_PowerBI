Zepto Analytics Dashboard: Data Visualization Project.

Objective:
Create a comprehensive dashboard for Zepto using Power BI, aiming to analyze key metrics and derive actionable insights to drive business decisions.

Steps:
1.Data Collection:
Gathered data from various reliable sources to ensure a thorough coverage of sales information.

2.Data Loading and Cleaning:
Imported the collected data into Power BI.
Cleaned the data by removing unnecessary columns and duplicates. Handling missing values. Changing data types as needed.

3.Data Analysis:
Used DAX functions, measures, and calculated columns for detailed analysis.

4.Key Metrics Analyzed:
Total Sales: $11.01M
Average Sales: $2.02K
Number of Orders: 5000
Average Customer Rating: 3.34

Insights:
1. Revenue vs Orders Insight
Total Revenue: ₹11.01 million from 5000 orders,
Average Order Value: ₹2.20K
This indicates customers are spending a healthy amount per order, showing effective pricing or bulk buying behavior.

2. Customer Payment Preferences
Card (₹2.87M) is the most preferred payment mode, closely followed by Cash, Wallet, and UPI.
Digital payments dominate overall, but cash still has a significant share (~25%), suggesting the need to support both online and offline payment options.

3. Top Product Categories
Leading categories by revenue share:
Dairy & Breakfast (11.32%)
Household Care (10.18%)
Pet Care (10.02%)
Baby Care is the lowest at (6.68%).
Focused marketing on high-margin or high-volume categories like Dairy and Household can enhance profitability.

4. Marketing ROI Efficiency
App marketing generated the highest revenue with the least proportional spend (efficient channel).
Email and SMS had similar revenue to others, but SMS had lower spend, making it more cost-effective than Social Media.
The business should consider reallocating budget from social to app and SMS marketing to optimize ROI.

5. Top Performing Areas
Orai (₹100K) and Deoghar (₹95K) are the highest sales contributors.
These areas can be targeted for pilot programs, faster delivery experiments, or new product launches.
